SMART 9, Power-On Hours. Background: the raw value is the total time the drive has spent in the powered-on state. The unit can be hours, minutes or seconds depending on the manufacturer. It records how long the drive has run; it is not an error count. Project use: context, and through derived features in the Isolation Forest. The rule baseline does not use it.
